# Privacy

FlashTitan is designed to stay mostly local to your PC.

## What FlashTitan may store

- app settings
- operation logs
- support bundles that you choose to create

## What FlashTitan may read

- image files you select
- removable-drive metadata used for safety checks
- checksum manifests for presets that provide them
- direct download URLs you paste into the app

## What FlashTitan does not try to do

- no account system
- no sign-in requirement
- no automatic log publishing
- no general-purpose web browsing inside the renderer

## When FlashTitan uses the network

FlashTitan uses the network only for tasks such as:

- downloading an image from a URL you provide
- opening an official vendor page
- fetching checksum text for presets that support it

## Support bundles

Support bundles can contain troubleshooting details such as:

- logs
- settings
- image metadata
- target metadata

Review a support bundle before sharing it.

## Plain-language summary

FlashTitan mostly works on your machine. If it goes online, it is usually because you asked it to download a file, open an official page, or fetch checksum information.
